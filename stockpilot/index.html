What it does

Your stockroom, in your pocket.

Inventory for small shops on Android. Scan barcodes, sell and receive, count shelves, reorder from suppliers, see profit. Offline, no account.

Scan to do anything

Point the camera at a barcode to find, sell or receive a product. A torch for dark shelves, and no double counts.

Every movement counts

Sell, receive, move between locations, write off, returns. Every change leaves a balance, so stock always adds up.

Reorder in a tap

See what's running low and turn it into one purchase order per supplier, sent as a message.

Stock counts

Count a shelf or a whole location by scanning. Sales during the count aren't mistaken for losses.

Know your numbers

Sales, profit and margin, best sellers, stock value by category, and what hasn't sold.

Mistakes are fixable

Undo a sale or delivery from the product's history. Back up and restore to a new phone.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

StockPilot isn't on the Play Store yet, so you install the APK directly. It takes three taps.

  1. Download the APKTap the button on your Android phone. You'll get stockpilot.apk.
  2. Allow the installAndroid asks before installing apps from outside the Play Store. Tap Settings, allow your browser (or Files app) to install apps, then go back.
  3. Open and installOpen the file from your notifications or Downloads and tap Install. Play Protect may ask you to confirm: choose “Install anyway”. Then open StockPilot.

What you need

  • Android 8.0 or newer
  • A camera for barcode scanning (typing codes works too)
  • About 130 MB free space
