What it does

Everything a small team needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Project and task management for small teams, made for phones. Boards, assignments, @mentions, live sync and offline mode.

Boards your way

A column for every step of your workflow, or start from Simple, Team, Content or Bugs. Long-press a card to move it.

Built for teams

Invite people with a 10-character code, set roles, and switch between workspaces.

Talk it through

Comments in chat bubbles, with @mentions that land in the right person's inbox.

Never miss a thing

Push notifications when you're assigned, mentioned, or a task you made is done.

Works offline

Changes are saved on the phone first and sync the moment you're back online. Nothing is lost or applied twice.

Satisfying to finish

Tap the big round checkbox: it pops, sends a burst of confetti and buzzes.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

FlowTask isn't on the Play Store yet, so you install the APK directly. It takes three taps.

  1. Download the APKTap the button on your Android phone. You'll get Flowstack.apk.
  2. Allow the installAndroid asks before installing apps from outside the Play Store. Tap Settings, allow your browser (or Files app) to install apps, then go back.
  3. Open and installOpen the file from your notifications or Downloads and tap Install. Play Protect may ask you to confirm: choose “Install anyway”. Then open FlowTask.

What you need

  • Android 8.0 or newer
  • Connects to a FlowTask server to sync with your team (you can point it at your own on the sign-in screen)
  • About 110 MB free space
