What it does

A music player, for your desktop.

Everything in Wallora works like the music app you already know: a library, playlists, a queue, a player bar. Only it's pictures.

Playlists

Shuffle, skip, repeat.

Group wallpapers into playlists, shuffle them, change every 30 minutes or once a day, and skip ahead from the tray.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

Wallora comes as a standard Windows installer. Here's the one extra click you'll need.

  1. Download the installerClick the button to get Wallora-1.1.0-x64.exe.
  2. Get past SmartScreenThe installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may say it “protected your PC”. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Install and goFollow the installer. Wallora lands in your Start menu and tray, and you can pin it to the taskbar.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • About 400 MB free space
  • Internet only for online wallpapers and weather
